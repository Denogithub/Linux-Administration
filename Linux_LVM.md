## Logical Volume Management (LVM)—implemented in modern Linux as LVM2 via the kernel device mapper—creates a flexible storage layer between your physical disks and your filesystems. Instead of rigid partitions tied to a single drive, you pool storage and carve out virtual volumes that can grow, shrink, or move without downtime. ##
